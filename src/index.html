OBSERVATION GUIDE

VIEWING TUBES

The Black Wheel Instrument is designed to be observed through viewing tubes.

The viewing tubes isolate a small region of the rotating field and make the organization easier to perceive.

The Phantom Axis Illusion button guides you directly to this observational region.

It is a guide for investigation with the viewing tubes.

It is not a replacement for the viewing tubes.

VIEWING ANGLE

>

Observe the display at approximately 45°–75° for the strongest experience.

Different viewing angles reveal different aspects of the organization while remaining within the same observational region.

This viewing angle is essential for the intended observation.

If using a laptop, tilt the display fully backward.

Observe above the Center and below the upper edge of the wheel through the viewing tubes.

If using a fixed desktop display, position yourself so you observe the display at approximately the same viewing angle.

This viewing angle optimizes the southern orientation used by the Black Wheel Instrument.

The Phantom Axis Illusion occurs only within this southern observational region.

LAPTOP • DESKTOP • iPAD

Use two standard cardboard toilet paper rolls lightly glued together with a small dab of glue at the rim of one end.

Align the tubes carefully and allow the glue to dry standing upright on a flat surface with the glued rims downward. This keeps the tubes evenly aligned.

Once dry, the binocular viewing tubes can be gently spread at the unglued end, allowing them to fit the face comfortably.

To construct the viewing tubes, you will need:

  • Two cardboard toilet paper rolls
  • Any glue

This construction works extremely well.

iPHONE

I recommend using one viewing tube.

The viewing distance is generally too narrow for comfortable binocular viewing when using standard toilet paper rolls.

Recommended

A rolled construction-paper tube approximately:

  • 6 inches long
  • 1–1¼ inches inside diameter

This provides the greatest comfort and the widest range of observation.

Binocular viewing tubes may also be constructed using two of these recommended construction-paper tubes.

Two equally sized tubes lightly glued together at one end, like the toilet-paper-roll binoculars, can also work wonderfully. Tube length may require slight adjustment from person to person.

Also Works Very Well

  • One standard cardboard paper towel roll
  • Two standard cardboard toilet paper rolls taped together end-to-end
  • One standard cardboard toilet paper roll also works surprisingly well

A single toilet paper roll provides a shorter viewing distance while still producing an excellent observation.

Two toilet paper rolls taped together end-to-end provide a longer viewing tube and generally produce the best results using common household materials.

Tape is recommended when rolling your own tubes because both the tube length and diameter can be adjusted easily while experimenting. Tube length is easily modified with scissors. Tape is fast, secure, and easy to modify.

Use in a very dark room.

To observe the Phantom Axis Illusion, look directly above the Center while keeping the Center outside the circular viewing aperture.

Also keep the upper edge of the wheel outside the circular viewing aperture.

For your first observation, use the Phantom Axis Illusion button to locate the region, then turn off the mask.

The organization remains present at every level of screen zoom within this region.

Under the proper viewing conditions, the organization appears immediately.

External visual cues compete with the organization for perceptual dominance.

Observe the same device from above or from either side, and the original rotation immediately returns.

When the perceptual system commits to the organization, the Phantom Axis Illusion becomes embodied.

The rotating points continue exactly as before.

CONTROL OBSERVATION

To observe only the original turning of the wheel, simply observe any other location on the wheel through the viewing tubes while maintaining the same viewing orientation.

If viewing the wheel on a laptop, first locate the Phantom Axis Illusion region. Without changing your position, slowly tilt the display toward you. The original rotation immediately returns.

The Phantom Axis Illusion is directional and polarized within the rotating field.

WHERE YOU'VE SEEN THIS BEFORE

The Phantom Axis Illusion is not unique to the Black Wheel.

The Black Wheel simply isolates an organization that already appears throughout ordinary experience.

Once recognized, it becomes surprisingly difficult to overlook.

The Black Wheel does not create this organization.

It reveals it.

The instrument allows it to be examined deliberately.

PASSING A TREE

While riding in a moving vehicle, direct your attention toward the ground near the base of a passing tree.

The organization appears immediately.

The organization remains centered near the base of the tree.

The field does not reorganize.

Only the observer’s interpretation of the surrounding motion reorganizes.

Once recognized, this organization becomes surprisingly familiar.

We seldom notice it because of its familiarity.

The mind accepts it.

It is simply the way motion appears.

ROTATIONAL MOTION

When the observer remains stationary and the field rotates, the organization no longer appears perpendicular to motion.

Instead, it appears as a radial organization extending from the center toward the edge of the rotating field.

The Black Wheel isolates this organization.

The Phantom Axis Illusion is the observer's recognition of it.

CAMERA MOVEMENT

Orbital camera movements frequently produce the same organization.

Once recognized, the Phantom Axis Illusion becomes increasingly difficult to overlook during ordinary camera movement.

OBSERVATIONAL LOCALITY

The organization does not occur everywhere.

It appears only within specific observational localities where relative motion is available to observation.

The Black Wheel allows these localities to be explored deliberately.

The Phantom Axis Illusion is one such locality.

RECOGNITION

The Black Wheel does not teach the organization.

It teaches the observer how to recognize it.

Recognition changes nothing in the field.

Recognition changes the observer.
